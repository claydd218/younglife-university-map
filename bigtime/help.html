Admin Help

How to use the Young Life University International admin tool.

Updates typically show up on the map in about 30 seconds — a small badge in the bottom-right corner tracks this after every save, so you don't have to guess. It reads "Publishing changes…" until your edit is actually live, then "Changes are live". You can keep working while it's up, or dismiss it with the ×.

Ministries Tab

Lists every ministry, grouped by division (Africa, Asia Pacific, Europe, etc.) in the same colors the public map uses for each region.

Searching and filtering

  • The search box matches city, country, or staff name as you type; the × button clears it.
  • Recent, No Staff, No Universities, No Ministry Photo, and Developing are toggle filters above the list — click one to narrow the list to matching ministries, click again to turn it off. Multiple filters combine together (e.g. Recent + No Staff shows only ministries that are both), and the list starts unfiltered.
  • Recent matches any ministry saved in the last 24 hours — that includes a staff member being moved into or out of it, not just a direct edit.

Adding or editing a ministry

  • + Add New Ministry opens a blank form; clicking Edit on an existing row opens it pre-filled.
  • New ministries get Save and Cancel buttons — Cancel discards everything, nothing is saved.
  • Editing an existing ministry instead opens on a single Cancel button — tapping it with nothing changed just closes the dialog. The moment you change anything (a field, a staff/university row, a photo), it switches to Update for the rest of that edit, since a photo add/remove/reorder is already saved the instant it happens and needs the ministry's record updated to match.
  • Save/Update stays disabled until City, Country, Latitude, and Longitude are all filled in — those four are what the public map needs to actually place the pin.
  • As you type into City, a dropdown suggests matching real place names to help catch typos and alternate spellings — pick one or keep typing your own, it's only a suggestion. Fill in Country first for more relevant matches.

Finding a location

  • Look Up Latitude and Longitude from City & Country geocodes the typed City/Country and fills in both fields. If it can't find that exact city, it falls back to the approximate center of the country instead of leaving the fields blank.
  • Once Latitude and Longitude have any value, an Adjust Pin Placement button appears. Click it to open a small map — drag the pin or click anywhere on the map to fine-tune the exact spot, and the Latitude/Longitude fields update to match.

Video link

The optional Video Link field takes a YouTube or Vimeo URL; Preview opens it the same way a visitor would see it, in an overlay on the map. Video Link Text is what visitors click on the map — leave it blank and it defaults to "Watch a [City] Story"; type your own to override it.

Ministry photos

A ministry can have several photos. + Add Photo uploads a new one — drag and drop one or several files at once, or use Choose File. The arrows reorder the list; the first photo is the "main" one — it's what shows in the map popup, and the rest appear when a visitor taps the photo to open the carousel. Remove deletes a photo from the live site immediately, no confirmation step beyond the prompt.

Staff and Universities

  • + Add Staff Member / + Add University add a new row with a Name/Role or Name/Year pair. Each staff row has its own profile photo widget, same drag-and-drop/Choose File pattern as the city photo.
  • The ↑ / ↓ arrows on each row reorder staff and universities independently — this controls the order they're listed in on the public map popup.
  • Move… on a staff row relocates that person to a different ministry: it opens a searchable list of every ministry, and on confirmation the staff member is removed from this ministry and added to the one you picked. Both ministries count as recently edited afterward.
  • Both kinds of row can be removed with the red Remove button, which asks for confirmation first — this can't be undone after the ministry is saved.

Images Tab

A single list of every staff photo across all ministries, grouped by division and country the same way the Ministries tab is, so it's easy to spot who's still missing a photo. Ministry photos aren't listed here — manage those from the ministry's own Edit dialog instead, since a ministry can have more than one.

  • Each entry shows a status dot: Good meets the recommended size, Low Resolution exists but is smaller than recommended, Missing has no photo at all.
  • Use the Good / Low Resolution / Missing filter buttons at the top to narrow the list down.
  • A name only opens a preview when it actually has a photo — click it to see the photo, click the enlarged version to close it again.
  • Missing entries get an Add Photo button. Existing ones get Remove instead — to replace a photo, remove it first and a fresh Add Photo button takes its place.

Recommended sizes: profile (staff) photos should be at least 400×400px and roughly square; ministry photos should be at least 1200×900px (4:3) — matches a standard landscape iPhone photo, so it doesn't need cropping to meet the bar.
